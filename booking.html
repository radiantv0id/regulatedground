SESSIONS & PRICING

Intro Call Free

A short 15-minute call to get to know your situation, answer your questions, and see whether working together would be a good fit. This is a conversation and not an assessment or practice session.

Initial Consultation and Assessment 75 EUR

In the first session we look at your history, your goals, the current state of your body-mind, and discuss a practice plan tailored to your needs. If you are having kundalini-related issues, a thorough assessment of the phase, route and blockages of your process will be done. Based on it you will be given a roadmap to regulate the process and support its unfolding.

Follow-Up Sessions 75 / 95 EUR (60 / 75 min)

Yoga Therapy

Practice-based: movement, breathing, and guided meditation/relaxation, followed by discussion and home-practice adjustments.

Personal Guidance

Conversation-based: coaching and integration support, processing what is arising, finding meaning and alignment.
