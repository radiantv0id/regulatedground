REBUILDING THE GROUND
BENEATH YOU

An Embodied Approach to Navigating Intensity

If you're living with unmanageable dysregulation, energy, pain, or sensitivity, this space is for you. Whether that takes the form of spiritual crisis, kundalini syndrome, fibromyalgia, trauma, or a body that's been running on overdrive too long, the underlying pattern is the same: a nervous system that has lost its safe ground and is no longer able to contain what moves through it or adapt to the demands of life.

With the help of yoga therapy that is tailored to your constitution, lifestyle and unique needs, you will learn to rebuild that ground and restore your body-mind's natural functioning and your capacity to engage life with more ease.

If you are in the midst of a spiritual crisis right now, whether induced through meditation, psychedelics, or natural causes, know that you are not alone. You are not broken and have not failed. What you are experiencing is valid and deeply meaningful. You are standing at the threshold of becoming more whole, human, and deeply yourself.

I am here to walk alongside you, helping you with body-oriented and conversational guidance to regulate intensity, reconnect with your body's intelligence, and meet life from a sense of agency.

Two Entry Points - One Ground

SPIRITUAL CRISIS INTEGRATION & KUNDALINI ADAPTATION

A spiritual crisis or kundalini awakening can feel overwhelming, confusing, and even shameful when there is no context or understanding. Without guidance, fear and resistance often block the natural unfoldment, creating unnecessary suffering and disorientation. With the right support, however, these experiences can be reframed as meaningful processes of transformation.

Here, listening and alignment are cultivated rather than forcing or suppressing what is unfolding, while the body's capacity to hold and move energy is gradually strengthened. With your cooperation and the right conditions, kundalini can move toward a natural resolution.

REGENERATIVE LIVING &
FUNCTIONAL ADAPTATION

For people living with fibromyalgia, chronic pain, or trauma, and anyone whose physiology has been progressively imbalanced by modern living: learn to move from overwhelm to ease and resilience.

Cultivate a lifestyle that builds and seals energy rather than depletes it through unconscious behavior and unhealthy habits. By attuning yourself to natural biological rhythms and your true needs, while reducing the environmental and behavioral inputs that maintain chronic activation, lifestyle becomes medicine. This foundation allows other interventions to have a deeper effect, because the way you live is no longer working against them. This isn't about fixing you, but about restoring the natural conditions your body needs to do its own work.
