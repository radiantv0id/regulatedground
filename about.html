ABOUT

RAISING AWARENESS OF THE GIFTS AND CHALLENGES OF KUNDALINI

Welcome. My name is Nils, and my mission is to bring understanding to the process of kundalini and support those navigating its challenges. With that same approach, I also support people living with fibromyalgia, trauma and other forms of dysregulation.

For over a decade I struggled with kundalini syndrome. The world I had known had fallen apart and I was torn into pieces, again and again. I felt lost, confused, and hopeless. With no context for what was happening, I thought I had gone insane. Everything I tried to build crumbled. Debilitating pain made me withdraw from life. I was constantly on fire. And my mind felt like a battlefield of unseen forces.

After trying countless therapies and practices that either barely delivered results or worsened my condition, I had to develop a new method, one that finally helped me emerge from the crucible more whole and embodied. I know both the challenges and gifts of the process from within my very marrow — how what begins as a crisis can turn into initiation.

Today, I accompany others through their awakening and through the difficult work of restoring the physiological foundation that allows a shift from surviving to thriving. I offer grounded presence and reassurance that crisis and illness can be a gateway to a richer, more meaningful life.

Rather than a teacher or therapist, I am a companion walking beside you. My role is to hold safe and compassionate space, not diagnose or prescribe, reminding you to trust the guidance of your own heart.

My hope is to foster a broader recognition of kundalini awakening as a valid phenomenon, to prevent harm through misdiagnosis, unnecessary medication or pathologization, and restore trust in the deeper intelligence at work within transformation.

In times of profound global crisis, we need individuals enlivened by kundalini to help guide collective evolution.
