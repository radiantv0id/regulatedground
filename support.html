Support

Ways of working together and resources that support your process.

Spiritual crisis, chronic pain and dysregulation can all feel overwhelming, confusing, and isolating when there's no context to understand them. Without guidance, fear and resistance often block the body-mind's natural capacity to heal and reorganize, creating unnecessary suffering. With the right support, these experiences can be reframed as meaningful processes of transformation.

Finding meaning is key to integration. There is no universal interpretation of your experience and the task of defining it is yours alone. My role is to accompany you with safe space, practical tools, and compassionate guidance, while honoring that your journey unfolds in its own unique way.

What You'll Gain

  • A regulated nervous system and a felt sense of safety in your body
  • Grounding and containment for whatever is moving through you
  • Practical tools for pacing yourself through periods of intensity
  • Language and context to understand what you're experiencing
  • Support integrating shifts in consciousness into daily life
  • Greater self-agency and trust in your experience
  • A body chemistry that supports rather than intensifies your process

As you learn to align with and trust the intelligence of your own unfolding, you may discover its deeper purpose and find yourself moved toward a purposeful and creative life that expresses your individual essence.

Ways to Work Together

  • Personal Guidance — A safe space to be listened to and supported in exploring your experience, finding meaning, and discovering practical ways to navigate what you're going through.
  • Yoga Therapy — Practical methods tailored to your constitution, nervous system, and capacity, designed to help you ground, regulate, and integrate.
  • Group Circles — weekly gatherings that provide a supportive space to share experiences, feel seen and connect with others navigating transformation.

Community

The strangeness and stigma that often accompanies these experience can lead many to withdraw from others. At times we need to retreat from the world and its responsibilities to turn inward. Yet, prolonged isolation can make things more difficult, straining an already overwhelmed nervous system. It may also trap us in trauma loops or fantasy scenarios, leaving us with the painful feeling that we are broken or utterly alone in our struggle.

Often, what is needed most is simply a safe space where we feel seen and accepted. Human connection is a fundamental need that is deeply rooted in our psyche and biology. We are social beings and thrive when we belong, when we are mirrored by others and when we can contribute to the life around us. Without closeness and reflection, we may lose our sense of who we are.

How a Session Works

Each session is shaped individually around your current state and needs. The work combines two complementary movements: soothing symptoms and calming the nervous system to build stability and depth work, the gentle unwinding of patterns and stored stress. Capacity is built at the outer levels first, before deeper layers are touched. Soothing alone brings short-term relief but isn't enough on its own for lasting change. Once stability is established can depth work begin, and with it, real change.

Who This Is For

  • People navigating an overwhelming spiritual awakening, including kundalini processes
  • People destabilized from intense meditation or spiritual practice
  • People feeling lost, confused or out of balance after a psychedelic experience
  • People with fibromyalgia, chronic pain, or other psychosomatic complaints based on dysregulation
  • People whose physiology is burdened from chronic stress, trauma, or modern life

Who This Isn't For

  • If you're experiencing acute psychiatric crisis, unmanaged psychosis or have uncontrolled substance dependence, you may need medical care first to stabilize enough.
  • If you're seeking to willfully trigger a kundalini awakening or chase peak spiritual experiences, here you find the opposite route.
  • This work is a commitment to a lifelong process of adaptation and integration. If you are looking for a quick fix and are unwilling or unable to engage with change, this is the wrong place.
